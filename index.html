Brett Adams Workspace Integration

Brett Adams Workspace Integration is a personal software project built by Brett Adams, a software engineering student, to connect his own Google account to a personal AI coding assistant (Claude Code) that runs locally on his own computer.

Purpose: the integration lets Brett direct his AI assistant to send email, create files in Google Drive, manage Google Calendar events, and read and write Google Sheets data — all on his own behalf, as part of his personal productivity workflow. Access is authorized through Google's standard OAuth 2.0 flow.

This is a single-user tool: Brett is both the developer and the only user. It is not a publicly distributed product or service, and no other individuals use it or have access to it.
