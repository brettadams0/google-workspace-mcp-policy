Brett Adams Workspace Integration

Brett Adams Workspace Integration is a personal automation tool, built and used by one person: Brett Adams, a software engineering student. It connects Brett's own Google account to a personal AI coding assistant (Claude Code) running locally on his own computer, so that assistant can carry out everyday Google Workspace tasks on his behalf when he asks it to.

What the app does

When Brett gives an instruction to his AI assistant — for example "send this email" or "add this to my calendar" — the assistant calls this app, which then calls the relevant Google API using Brett's own authorized credentials to carry out that single action. Nothing happens automatically or in the background; every action is triggered directly by Brett.

Why each permission is requested

  • Gmail (send, compose): so Brett can ask his assistant to draft and send emails from his own Gmail account on his behalf.
  • Drive (drive.file): so Brett can ask his assistant to create new files in his own Google Drive. This scope only grants access to files the app itself creates, not Brett's existing Drive files.
  • Calendar (events, read-only): so Brett can ask his assistant to view his schedule and create, update, or remove events on his own calendar.
  • Sheets: so Brett can ask his assistant to read and update data in his own Google Sheets.

Who uses it

This is a single-user tool. Brett is both the developer and the only user — it is not a publicly distributed product or service, it is not marketed or offered to anyone else, and no other individuals use it or have access to it.
